ABOUT

About this prototype

SapphireUSX is a non-diagnostic interface prototype.

About our team

We’re a team interested in technology, health data, and space exploration. We created SapphireUSX as a prototype to explore how astronaut health information could be presented clearly and be easy to understand. Our team chose the project direction and worked together to review and refine the interface.

AI tools

We used ChatGPT for project guidance, GitHub Copilot as a coding assistant, and Claude for research assistance. Our team made the design and project decisions and reviewed and refined AI-assisted suggestions.

Prototype disclaimer

This prototype is not for health monitoring, diagnosis, or treatment decisions. All displayed readings are synthetic demo values.
